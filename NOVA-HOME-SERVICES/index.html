NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services.

What services does Nova provide?

Nova Home Services covers home cleaning, everyday household repairs and garden care.

How can I request a service?

Complete the booking form, select your service and provide the requested details.

Can I choose my preferred service date?

Yes. You can enter a preferred date in the booking form. The date is a request and is not confirmed until arrangements are made.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. Submitting a request does not automatically confirm an appointment. The booking process requires follow-up and confirmation.

How can I explain a special service request?

Use the additional details field in the booking form to describe your requirements.
